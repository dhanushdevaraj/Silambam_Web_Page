ACADEMY SYLLABUS

Comprehensive Silambam Curriculum

From foundational footwork to traditional warrior weaponry. A progressive, scientifically grounded syllabus preserving centuries of martial heritage.

Traditional Level & Belt Progression

⚪ Level 1: White

Salutations (Vanakkam), Silambam history, Kaalvarisai 1–4, stick grip & stance balance.

🟡 Level 2: Yellow

Kaalvarisai 5–8, continuous rotational swinging (Vetukol), single-stick defensive parries.

🟢 Level 3: Green

Full 18 Footwork variations, dual-handed twin sticks (Kurunkambu), introductory sparring.

🟤 Level 4: Brown

Competitive state tournament combat, Maduvu (Deer Horns) trapping, counter-attacking.

⚫ Level 5: Black / Ashan

Surul Vaal (Flexible sword), Sword & Shield, Varma Kalai pressure points, teaching certification.
