ANCIENT TAMIL MARTIAL TRADITION

The Living Heritage of Sage Agastya & Tamil Kings

Silambam (சிலம்பம்) is one of the world's oldest martial arts, documented in ancient Sangam literature over three millennia ago. Practiced by Chola, Chera, and Pandya warriors, it uses high-speed bamboo staff manipulation and intricate footwork (Kaalvarisai) to neutralize attackers in seconds.

At {{ academy.name }}, we preserve this lineage without watering down its lethal speed, mental focus, and spiritual depth. Every student learns respectful traditions, body conditioning, and total mastery of weapon and mind.

SACRED WEAPONRY

The Arsenal of Silambam

Each weapon develops a distinct neurological pathway, balance, and warrior philosophy.

நெடுங்கம்பு

Nedunkambu (Bamboo Staff)

The core soul of Silambam. Measured to reach the practitioner’s forehead. Delivers strikes exceeding 150 km/h with continuous rotational momentum.

Length: 1.68m (5.5 ft) Material: Kizhi Bamboo
குறுங்கம்பு

Kurunkambu (Double Sticks)

Short fighting batons used in dual hands simultaneously. Builds equal ambidexterity, defensive parries, and ultra-fast close-quarter traps.

Length: 60 cm (2 ft) Pair: Two Hands
சுருள் வாள்

Surul Vaal (Flexible Ribbon Sword)

The most dangerous and awe-inspiring weapon. Coiled flexible steel blades that whip and slice in continuous hypnotic arcs, creating an impenetrable sphere.

Reach: 1.5m whip Skill: Grandmaster Level
மடுவு / மான்கொம்பு

Maduvu (Deer Horns)

Ancient close-combat parrying weapon crafted from naturally shed deer horns tipped with brass/steel spikes, designed to lock enemy weapons and counter-strike.

Type: Defensive & Hooking Heritage: Sangam Era
