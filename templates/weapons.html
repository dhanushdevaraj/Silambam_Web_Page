TRADITIONAL ARSENAL

Weapons of Ancient Tamil Warriors

In Silambam tradition, weapons are revered as sacred extensions of the human nervous system. Explore the weapons taught at {{ academy.name }}.

1. நெடுங்கம்பு — Nedunkambu (Long Bamboo Staff)

Core Weapon

The primary weapon of Silambam. Made of seasoned Kizhi or Male Bamboo treated with herbal oils to achieve maximum flexibility without splintering. The staff is measured individually from the student’s toes to their eyebrows.

Length: 5.5 to 6 Feet (1.68m)
Key Movements: Vetukol (Chop), Thadupukol (Block), Kuthukol (Thrust)
Prerequisite: Beginner onwards

2. குறுங்கம்பு — Kurunkambu (Double Short Sticks)

Intermediate

Two matching wooden sticks held in either hand. The practitioner executes rapid alternating strikes and blocks, training both sides of the brain to operate with equal lightning velocity.

Length: 2 to 2.5 Feet (60 cm)
Key Movements: Dual-wielding rotational cross-blocks, neck and knee traps
Prerequisite: Yellow Belt

3. சுருள் வாள் — Surul Vaal (Flexible Ribbon Sword)

Master Level Only

One of the most lethal and respected weapons in ancient warfare. Consists of multiple razor-sharp flexible steel strips fastened to a handle. The momentum of the sword must never stop during execution; if momentum pauses, the user risks slicing themselves. Taught strictly after years of discipline.

Blade Count: 1 to 4 Flexible Spring Blades
Application: Defensive ring against cavalry or surrounding infantry
Prerequisite: Black Belt / Ashan authorization

4. மடுவு / மான்கொம்பு — Maduvu (Deer Horns)

Advanced Heritage

Traditionally constructed from shed blackbuck horns linked by a central grip and fortified with metal points. Used for parrying long swords or spears, trapping the opponent’s weapon between the horns, and delivering instant counters.

Composition: Dual opposing horns with hand shield / guard
Function: Close-range disarmament & body locks
Prerequisite: Brown Belt

Ethical Oath of Silambam

"The weapons of Silambam are bestowed solely for protection, defense of the weak, and preservation of righteousness. Never for aggression or cruelty."
